# Product qualification rules

- Marketplace: eBay UK, GBP, fixed-price comparisons preferred.
- Selling price: at least £50.
- Demand: three distinct sellers, each with at least 20 verified units in the rolling 30-day period; at least 60 total.
- Supplier: matched AliExpress listing has at least 500 orders, ships to the UK, and has the selected variation available.
- Reputation: at least 4.5/5 when the source explicitly provides a five-star seller metric. Positive-feedback percentage retains its own scale and threshold.
- Profit: positive net profit; 20% target net margin after fees, advertising, returns, taxes/duties, and operating costs.
- Exclude beauty/cosmetics/skincare/makeup. Automotive is a ranking preference, not a mandatory restriction.
- Confirmed model, fitment, pack, kit, accessory-only, or selected-variation mismatch is a rejection.
- Missing mandatory evidence produces `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`, never `QUALIFIED`.
