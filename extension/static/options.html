Hunter Companion

Reads exact 30-day sold counts from eBay purchase history using your own signed-in eBay session, and skips AliExpress bundle-deal pages.

Before you start

Sign in to eBay in this Chrome profile. If eBay shows a sign-in page or a bot check, the extension pauses for 6 hours and shows a red “!” badge. Sign in or solve it on ebay.com, then press Resume in the popup.
